Dockhand

What you need to get started:

1
Your Dockhand URL
Open Dockhand in your browser and copy the address including the port.
Example: http://192.168.1.100:3001
2
Your username and password — only if authentication is enabled
These are the credentials you use to log in to Dockhand.
Leave them empty if authentication is disabled in Dockhand.
Authentication disabled? Just fill in the URL and leave username and password empty.
